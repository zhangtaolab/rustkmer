<objective>
Replace per-window O(length times k) encoding with a rolling 2-bit encoder and a word-level reverse-complement for k at most 32, proving the emitted k-mer sequence is identical to the existing encoder on the full edge matrix.

Purpose: The profiling microbench measured the rolling encode plus bit-parallel canonicalization at 70.9 ns per window versus 190.8 ns for the production path, a 2.7x single-thread lever. A rolling encoder that fails to reset at N or at a record boundary would silently recreate the stale-window chimeric k-mer defect, so the differential edge matrix is the correctness gate.

Output: Rolling encoder and bit-parallel reverse-complement in the k-mer core, the CLI and PyO3 hot loops wired to it for k at most 32 with the u128 path retained for larger k, and a k 1-through-64 differential plus performance gate.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries
