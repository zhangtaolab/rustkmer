<objective>
Move counting accumulation to per-worker thread-local tables with a checked end-of-file reduction, removing per-window global atomics while keeping counts exactly equal on both surfaces.

Purpose: The Phase 5 contention evidence attributes 120.22 ns per window (about 115 percent of the shared-versus-local gap) to the per-window `total_kmers` and `unique_kmers` fetch_add operations. Removing them from the hot path is the contention lever, and the reduction becomes a new fail-loud summation site that must preserve the exact u32 count contract.

Output: A local accumulator type and KmerCounter reduction API, the CLI hot loop rewired to it, PyCounter inheriting it, and a differential/overflow test suite proving exactness.
</objective>
