<objective>
Produce the phase's CLI evidence at full human scale and stop at Checkpoint 1: the committed slice gate must be green, then the CLI binary is measured over the complete CRR1936095 read-1 set (both split parts) at k=21 and k=31, both against jellyfish at matched 16 threads with a t1 scaling arm.

Purpose: this is the first expensive wave — roughly 2 to 2.5 hours of machine time — and it is deliberately separate from every harness edit. The Python surface is not touched here. If any gate fails, the sequence stops and the failure is recorded; no production file is edited to turn it green.

Measured facts this plan plans against (from RESEARCH.md, measured/extrapolated): 69,082,640 reads total across both parts; per-rep wall about 145-180 s for `rk-t16`, about 9 minutes for `rk-t1`, about 166-233 s for `jf-t16`; projected CLI peak 68-80 GB at k=21; 242 GiB free disk against a 150 GiB floor. Because both rustkmer arms write a 21-24 GB `.rkdb` and jellyfish a 10-14 GB `.jf`, each k's scratch databases are deleted after their stats and sha256 are recorded; otherwise the second k would trip the disk floor.

The t1 arm runs in the same counterbalanced block but gets no separate warmup (the rustkmer warmup covers the tool) and is excluded from the acceptance rule.

Output: `10-slice-gate.json`, `10-results-full-k21.json`, `10-results-full-k31.json`, and a Checkpoint-1 report delivered to the user before Plan 10-04 begins.
</objective>
